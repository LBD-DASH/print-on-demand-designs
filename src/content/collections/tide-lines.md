Tide Lines repeats a single wave stroke in alternating colours. The pattern tiles easily, which suits mug wraps and sticker sheets.

## Where it could work

- **Mugs:** a wave band around the barrel.
- **Posters:** five or more rows, with room for a short typographic line.
- **T-shirts:** a narrow stripe across the chest.

This is a concept for feedback and is not on sale.
