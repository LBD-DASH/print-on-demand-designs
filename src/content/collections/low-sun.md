Low Sun is built from one idea: a circle and a few evenly spaced rays. The shapes are intentionally plain so they print cleanly at small sizes on stickers and at large sizes on posters.

## Where it could work

- **Posters:** the large central circle gives a calm focal point.
- **Stickers:** one bold circle reads well at small sizes.
- **Mugs and T-shirts:** the ray ring works as a chest print or a wraparound band.

Final colours will depend on the print method a provider uses, so this palette is a starting point only.
